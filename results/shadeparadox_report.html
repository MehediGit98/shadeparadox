A blind that descends only as far as the glare source requires costs little circadian exposure, because the sun and the bright sky sit in the upper band of the aperture. The cost appears when the blind is operated the way people actually operate blinds — closed readily, reopened rarely.

Encodings

keymeaning
glare_onlyMinimise the fraction of occupied hours above DGP 0.35. Nothing else enters.
energy_onlyMinimise cooling plus lighting electricity.
glare_energyEqual weight on glare exceedance and energy. The encoding most shading papers actually use.
circadian_onlyMaximise the fraction of occupied hours at or above 250 lx melanopic EDI.
coupled_linearEqual weight on glare, energy and circadian. An editorial choice, stated as one.
coupled_constraintMaximise threshold coverage with a soft glare penalty; energy breaks ties.
